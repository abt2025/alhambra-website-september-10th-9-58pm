## 1. Introduction

This document provides a comprehensive overview of the AWS architecture for the Alhambra Bank & Trust digital banking platform. The architecture is designed to be secure, scalable, and resilient, leveraging a wide range of AWS services to deliver a modern, feature-rich banking experience. The platform includes advanced features such as AI-powered video KYC, multi-language support, and a zero-trust security model.

## 2. Guiding Principles

The architecture is guided by the following principles:

- **Security First:** Implementing a zero-trust security model and leveraging AWS security services to protect sensitive customer data.
- **Scalability & Performance:** Designing a scalable architecture that can handle a growing number of users and transactions without compromising performance.
- **High Availability & Resilience:** Ensuring the platform is highly available and resilient to failures by deploying across multiple Availability Zones.
- **Automation & DevOps:** Automating the deployment and management of the infrastructure to improve efficiency and reduce human error.
- **Cost-Effectiveness:** Optimizing the use of AWS services to minimize costs while meeting the performance and security requirements.
